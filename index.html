V0.4 TOOLKIT

More than an installer.

BlackForge adds guardrails and evidence around pacman. Pacman remains the final package authority.

01

Security audit

Separate outdated, vulnerable, unavailable, and upstream-maintenance states. Optionally reads official Arch advisories through arch-audit.

blackforge audit
02

Locks + SBOMs

Capture exact versions and cached archive checksums. Export CycloneDX or SPDX JSON.

blackforge lock sbom
03

Rollback planning

Detect Btrfs/Snapper, take opt-in snapshots, and locate exact packages in pacman's cache.

--snapshot
04

Reviewed presets

Share data-only collections with source-qualified packages—never executable hooks.

blackforge community
05

AUR discovery

Opt in to official AUR metadata. BlackForge never downloads or executes a PKGBUILD.

aur --enable-aur
06

Maintenance dashboard

Create a portable HTML report with historical catalog observations, availability, stale and archived evidence.

blackforge dashboard build
07

Native Linux fit

Man page, shell completion, optional disabled systemd timer, PackageKit-style JSON, and Turkish quick help.

blackforge help --lang tr

SAFETY MODEL

Clarity before privilege.

BlackForge resolves only known package names, shows exact transactions, relies on pacman's signed repositories, and makes risky integrations explicit. Security tools are for systems you own or are authorized to test.
